A phase-changing reality

Where each piece of the theory stands, which paper carries it, and what comes next.

Emily Smith · Charlottesville · analysis drafted with Claude (Anthropic)

The claim, in the author's words

  1. X is stable for now.
    • Our spacetime is one settled arrangement of something deeper, X: a specific, relatively stable arrangement with its own dimensions, curled where ours are open.
    • X was stable for now, not for ever. Most likely it was a slab, two directions already open and one still curled tight.
    • The directions curl separately, each behind its own wall, so the slab's one curled direction releases the whole burp when it opens.
  2. Energy arrives, and the curl gives way: the burp.
    • Energy arriving in X, as a black hole's infall arrives, raises its excitation.
    • When the stores first hold the whole cost of the curl, the curled direction gives way: the burp, a fixed release of energy.
  3. The burp becomes the Big Bang's hot lump.
    • The release is the hot lump: light, with matter and antimatter in almost equal amounts.
    • As it cooled, nearly every pair of matter and antimatter cancelled into more light, so light carried almost all of the energy.
    • Every daughter starts with the same amount, the burp's release; this curling energy is released only then. The release is fast, a far bigger explosion than any bomb. The matter we are made of is the small excess that does not cancel, about one part in a billion: from light turned into matter, from the two kinds' unequal prices, or from the network's curvature tipping the balance, hypotheses to test.
    • While the light has nowhere to go it is pressed back into matter, and matter is curved space: tiny loops that are only a relationship, no points, metastable, which build quarks and then protons; once there is room, the light leaves instead.
  4. The release leaves one way, and X re-forms.
    • The release goes into the daughter; nothing passes from open space back into X.
    • The slab re-forms and goes on accepting energy, so the black hole is a converter, not a store.
  5. What did not convert is the scrap.
    • Small knots of fixed energy, made while the new space is still hot enough to make flaws, then fixed.
    • The scrap is the best current guess for the dark matter; whether it is the dark matter is open.
  6. Dark energy is the energy of space itself.
    • A candidate source, adopted as a hypothesis to test: the network's own thermal defect density, the few defects any network carries at a coupling above zero.
    • The coupling is the loop's, set by the loop's total energy once every daughter's energy has returned to the network at the end of a turn.
  7. The books balance, and the rules hold.
    • Energy is conserved across the change.
    • The rules of the two phases are their constants and do not change from one generation to the next.
  8. Black holes curl space back, and each one births many daughters.
    • Inside the space that forms, black holes are regions where the parent's own geometry re-curls, two directions still open and one curled tight.
    • A daughter space opens while the black hole remains in the parent, and every black hole births many new ones, so the tree of universes multiplies each generation. A black hole is bigger inside than it looks: each daughter is the size its mother was at the same time since its own birth.
    • A turn is one universe's own life, from its burp until the last of its black holes has dissolved; the end is many black holes, carried apart by the cold, expanding void, each dissolving alone.
  9. Each universe keeps its own clock.
    • Every universe runs an exact copy of one clock, from its own birth to its own heat death; no clock runs across universes.
    • Universes of one setting that differ only by a quantum outcome are counted each as a whole.
  10. The quantum world is versions not yet told apart.
    • An arrangement's undetectably different versions all stand at once, until an interaction makes them distinguishable.
    • The versions are the branches of the many-worlds reading; the rule that lets them cancel is the published one: versions carry amplitudes, and a record in the surroundings keeps them apart.

The tests use a toy: a network whose rule is a published model of emergent geometry (Trugenberger's) plus a cost for curling tight; since 18 October the version that stands in for the black hole also lets points compact in whole layers instead of breaking, with a count of points that varies. With those terms it is a close cousin of his model, not his model. A toy can show a mechanism is possible or impossible in a class of models; that reality works this way is the author's claim. Its energy is written term by term below, with the source of each term.

No physicist has reviewed any of this yet. Paper 1 is with the model's author, asking him to read it. Both its editions are at sidenerdapps.com/mainnerd.

The toy, term by term

Every test uses one energy, built from terms added one at a time. A run switches on the terms it needs; the energy is their sum. Each term says where it came from: published (taken from a paper, equation named), ours (added by this programme, with its dated decision), or planned. Written for the four-link toy; with six links the first term reads 16(3N − S).

N points, S small squares, X surplus squares (each link on a third square counts one), λ the curling knob (1.25 in most runs; at λ = 1 the toy is Trugenberger's model), d a point's open directions, φ soft space's number on a point, ep the curling energy at a point, Ah and δh a hinge's size and how far the angles around it fall short of flat.

Where it stands

A torus with one direction curled, the toy's stand-in for X, sits still, then changes from one spot. The change runs as a front, like a flame through paper, and gives up the same energy per point every time: the burp. The push that starts it is a single small move of fixed cost at every size: a match lights a campfire or a forest.

What the tube opens into is still a tube, its seam re-glued so the way around is six steps where it was four, not a wide sheet. The toy's rule cannot tell the two apart, so nothing pays for growing wider. With an added price on loops of six, the tube does come out mostly wide, though crumpled with flaws throughout.

Where it stands

The author X is the slab, two directions open and one curled tight, likely with relics inside it; the directions curl separately, each behind its own wall. The opening is started not by one spark but by energy that keeps arriving, as a black hole's infall does, until the stores first hold the whole cost of the curl.

In three directions, one push has never opened all of X: the directions open one at a time, a combination lock rather than a zipper. Under the published move, which breaks squares, the slab did not open at any size tried: given one spark it fell back in 47 runs of 48 (T65); fed at a fixed rate it broke or sat, never a front (T67); fed at a rate fixed, ramped or pulsed, into one store, one point, sixteen points or every point, at three curling costs, it broke or sat in every one of 2,592 runs (T68). Exact: the cheapest clean opening out of the slab costs 64 and opens 16 points, two switches away, at every curling cost tried (T69). The published move cannot move the slab without first breaking four points.

The author, 18 Oct · decided The toy is adjusted for the black hole's inside: points do not break, they compact, two linked points collapsing into nothing and their neighbours joining up, so the count of points varies; the move acts on a whole layer at once (six around to four around and back), with no seam and no point broken; X is the straight curl, and the spiral she pictured is the seam of the product after the burp. A single switch is kept only as a control. Exact Under this form the opening is one move. The split of the slab is downhill and releases the whole curl, so X is stable for now only with a gate: the split fires only when the stores beside the cut already hold the slab's whole cost, her "a step when the stores first hold the whole cost", decided 19 Oct. The lump pays the fold straight back unless it leaves.

The author, 18 Oct · decided "It goes in its own daughter universe. Then the slab reforms and continues accepting energy. Nothing can go from open space to the slab, it's one way." Exact, from the kernel The parent is then a converter: every slab's worth of energy fed becomes one daughter of exactly that energy; at one small size, 61 daughters of 144 each over 600 sweeps, equal to the energy fed. The gate sets only how long X sits between re-forming and burping, not how many daughters there are or what each carries. Per point everything scales together, so the births fall at the same sweeps at every size for the same seed.

Measured, 21 Oct T71 ran the slab under the layer form with her gate, at three curling costs, two sizes and two feed rates. In every setting the slab cycles: it burps, re-forms and burps again, 40 to 340 daughters a run, each carrying exactly the slab's curling cost. The first burp comes when the stores first hold that cost, at the same moment at both sizes. The clean two-switch move fired only at the lowest curling cost with the faster feed, after the first burp: there the feed outran the toy's one layer move a sweep, the stores piled up, and daughters fell short of the full cost by 1 and 11 per cent. The single-switch control sat still. Her prediction held except on that one count; ours missed three times, each traced to our own reading of the protocol. What it shows is close to arithmetic: under the layer form the parent turns each slab's worth of energy into one daughter, as long as it keeps pace.

Measured, 21 Oct T74 fed the slab in meals through its edge, with one top speed for energy crossing it, then stopped feeding it and set it among colder, equal or warmer surroundings. Once the meals stop, a slab in colder surroundings gives its energy away (34 settings of 36) and one in warmer surroundings is fed and burps again (36 of 36). The burp's three shares

The author's best guess · dark energy given a candidate source · needs a physicistPaper 7Updated 21 Oct 2026
Where it stands

The author The burp is the hot lump, light and ordinary matter; the scrap is the dark matter; dark energy is the energy space itself keeps.

Ours At reality's birth nearly all the energy was the hot lump, so one opening has to carry nearly the whole burp. With the slab as X only one direction is left to open, so its single release is the whole burp. The scrap left in the toy is far more than birth can afford, so the dark-matter reading rests on how few starting points an opening can have. Flat space in the toy keeps no energy at all, which leaves today's small dark energy unexplained.

The author, 19 Oct · decided At the end of a turn every daughter's energy has returned to the network, and the network's temperature is fixed by the loop's total energy alone; so the loop's coupling is a derived number, the same every turn; no value of it has been computed. Adopted as a hypothesis to test: dark energy is the network's own thermal defect density at that coupling. Ours At any coupling above zero the network carries a few defects, because there are vastly more defected arrangements than one perfect lattice; the published model reads the negative curvature this gives a timeless network as a positive cosmological constant in spacetime, its dark energy, and lets it fall as the coupling runs. Hers freezes the coupling: fixed by the loop rather than by the matter's temperature, the defect density never thins as space grows and behaves as a cosmological constant. What is measured is a density, constant to about ten per cent over eight billion years; shares at any epoch follow from it. The toy cannot yet test this: every sealed run gives the network and the matter one temperature, so a kernel with a cold network and a hot daughter is the named next step, and a density that comes out small counts only after its constants are pinned by something other than the target.

The author, 20 Oct The network's temperature at the end, set by the loop's total energy, is the same number as empty space's, set by its dark energy. Ours Joined to her dark-energy hypothesis, that is one equation for one unknown. It has a solution if a defect costs about 146 times the end temperature: a price set at the scale of the cosmic horizon, not the Planck scale. So the old puzzle of why dark energy is so small becomes why a defect is so cheap, restated rather than solved.

Measured, 20 and 21 Oct The toy's stiff space cannot hold the thin, even, steady density of defects her dark energy would be, at any coupling, on the flat sheet or in the space a burp actually makes: defects come in patches that stay rare or grow into a melt. Grown row by row at the one coupling where the sheet holds a few patches without melting, the density thinned in some runs and climbed in others, constant in none. Soft space (piece 8) is the ingredient designed for it.

The author, 21 Oct The uncurl left behind when matter formed added to the network's own negative curvature, so dark energy did change early on, and some of that change is still in view; it is settling toward its constant, not falling to zero. Ours The toy already leans this way: in flat space every curl sits inside a larger uncurl, so making matter leaves extra uncurl. Recent surveys hint that dark energy has weakened over the last few billion years. To read as dark energy and not as extra matter, the excess has to heal back toward the steady density, not simply thin out. Testable in soft space, by giving it an excess and watching where it settles, and against the sky, where her picture says dark energy approaches its constant from above and never reaches zero.

Chapter 2. What the change leaves behind

7

The scrap: three kinds of knot of fixed energy

MeasuredPapers 2, 3, 7, 18Updated 20 Oct 2026
Where it stands

Not every point converts. What is left is a handful of small knots, each stuck in its own right: a column, a loop of four that still closes a square as the old tube did; a double column; and a knot of two kinds of point. How many is decided at the opening, not by the cooling after. Slow cooling heals the columns; the other two never heal. Most scraps sit free in the new space; about one in five is the joint between two widths of tube, like the stitch where a knitted sleeve changes its number of loops.

Ours That is the shape cosmologists call freeze-out: durable objects whose number is fixed by how the space formed.

The author, 18 Oct Could the scraps be the seeds of structure, the denser regions that collapsed into galaxies? A look, 18 Oct · not evidence Read from the 600 saved sheets, the knots sit clustered on a few columns and at random beyond, and where a knot sits after a slow cooling, one sat after a faster one nine times in ten: their positions are set at the opening. Equal knots scattered at random are shot noise, flat across scales, while the sky's seeds are nearly the same at every scale, so the scraps' own layout cannot be the seed pattern. What survives is scraps as dark matter falling into seeds laid by something else.

The author, 21 Oct Dark matter's flaws are made as space adds links, a regular sprinkling that then settles as a brain does: well-used paths thicken into filaments and galaxies, little-used ones fade into flat empty space. Ours The likeness between the cosmic web and the brain's network of neurons has been measured, and gravity grows the web much as she describes the paths. One measurement limits the making: dark matter has thinned exactly as matter does since the time of the microwave background, so it was not being made over that span; an early making is not ruled out.

The author, 21 Oct Curl is matter and uncurl antimatter; most of a particle's mass is the energy binding its loops, not the loops. A look, 21 Oct · exact within reach Matter and antimatter weigh the same, so the toy was asked whether it treats the two kinds as mirror images. It does not: an extra square costs a quarter of a missing one at the usual setting, and in flat space no flaw carries curl alone; every curl sits inside a larger uncurl. So the equal masses would have to come from what binds the loops, which the toy does not have yet.
