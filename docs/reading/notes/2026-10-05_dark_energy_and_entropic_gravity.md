# Disordered locality, and gravity from entropy: reading notes (4 to 5 October 2026)

Four papers were read in full by the assistant in the chat sessions of 4 and 5 October, and summarized in the hand-over
of those sessions (`docs/HANDOFF_2026-10-05_chat.md`, section 4). **These notes are that summary, put in order. The
papers were not read again in the repository session that filed them**, so every statement below rests on the chat
session's reading; whether the owner has read these passages is not recorded. Keys refer to `REFERENCES.bib`.
Bibliographic details are as the hand-over gives them, to verify against the catalogue.

## [PWS09] Prescod-Weinstein and Smolin, "Disordered locality as an explanation for the dark energy" (2009)

- **The idea** (Markopoulou's disordered locality): a network that is mostly local can keep a few links between points
  that are far apart in the emergent space. Such a link carries nearest-neighbour energy with nowhere local to go.
  Averaged over where the links sit, the energy is proportional to their number.
- **When it is dark energy:** if the number of such links grows with the comoving volume, the stress it contributes is
  proportional to the metric, pressure equal to minus the energy density.
- **What it needs:** about 10⁶⁰ such links (one end per roughly 100 km, each of about the Planck energy); the
  antiferromagnetic sign; growth with volume assumed, not derived; no unique signature. A proton lifetime of about
  10⁵¹ years comes out.
- **The rule this project takes from it:** energy held at a count that grows with the number of points behaves as dark
  energy; energy held at a fixed count thins out as matter does. So a frozen scrap is matter-like, and anything that is
  to be dark energy has to be per point.

## [Jac95] Jacobson, "Thermodynamics of spacetime: the Einstein equation of state" (1995)

- Einstein's equation as an equation of state: entropy proportional to horizon area (which needs a cutoff at the Planck
  scale), the Unruh temperature, and heat = temperature × change of entropy applied to every local horizon.
- The cosmological constant is left undetermined. The derivation fails out of local equilibrium (the Big Bang, the
  inside of a black hole). Its moral: do not quantize the metric, as one does not quantize sound.
- **For this project:** it needs causal horizons, so a model with relativity's time. It is the target claim 2 has to
  reproduce, not a tool this model can use.

## [Ver11] Verlinde, "On the origin of gravity and the laws of Newton" (2011)

- A force from entropy: F Δx = T ΔS; bits proportional to area; equipartition; a change of entropy of 2π per Compton
  wavelength; Newton's law, Poisson's equation and Einstein's follow; inertia is entropic too.
- **Said explicitly:** information stored at lattice points with no duplication gives no holography and no gravity.
- Heuristic. Objections on the record: neutron interference states (Kobakhidze); non-conservative forces (Visser,
  Hossenfelder).
- **For this project:** it works without relativity's time, so it is the usable relative. In two space dimensions the
  coefficient carries a factor d − 3 and there is no finite G, so a pull is tested in three directions only. An entropic
  force needs a temperature above zero, so the exact zero-temperature results (no force between leftovers at fixed
  wiring, O22) could not have shown one. The warm-bath pull should be designed as F = T dS/dr.

## [Ver16] Verlinde, "Emergent gravity and the dark universe" (2016)

- The area law comes from short-range entanglement of the units that build space; positive dark energy adds a thermal
  **volume-law** entropy (one unit per volume V0 = 4Għ L / (d − 1)); de Sitter space as a glassy, stuck-for-now ensemble.
- **Dark energy is the first phase and matter appears later by a transition out of the medium.** Matter removes entropy
  2πMr/ħ from an inclusion; where only part is removed the medium responds elastically, and the response looks like dark
  matter: a relation between the apparent dark and the ordinary surface densities with no free parameter (Tully–Fisher,
  with a0/6).
- **Regime:** isolated, spherical, static; no cosmology. **Tests:** weak lensing (Brouwer and others, 2016) consistent;
  isolated dwarf galaxies (2017) not; clusters mixed; disfavoured in its original form.
- **Lessons taken:** a count that grows with volume is the dark-energy side, not a failure; gravity still needs the
  area-law count from the links; and a claim with no free parameter that went to data within a year is the bar.
