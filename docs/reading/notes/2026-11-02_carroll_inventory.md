## The pieces, for reference

(1) space as a phase of a graph or network, emergent geometry; (2) a sharp birth from a stable-for-now state with energy released; (3) whether gravity is quantum and what carries it; (4) dark matter and dark energy as features of space; (5) a strange-loop cosmology with observers as the measure; (6) particles as loops; (7) time emergent from motion, the arrow of time; (8) self-reference, Everett and the observer.

Piece 6, particles as loops: **none found**, as the brief expected. His 1990s texture papers are topological defects, not loops as particles, and are left out.

## Tier 1: read in full (nine)

Ordered for reading. The 2026 cyclic paper and the entanglement-geometry line are at the top; their abstracts give no reason to move them.

1. **2605.30405**, Toward a Phenomenologically Acceptable Quantum Cyclic Universe. Carroll, Nadiia Diachenko, Saakshi Dulani. 2026. No journal given. Pieces 2, 5, 7. *Why (ours, from the abstract):* an exactly periodic quantum cosmology, periodic rather than merely recurrent when the energy-eigenvalue differences are commensurable, in which the evolution from minimum entropy can show an entropy excursion far larger than the usual P(ΔS) ∝ exp(−ΔS), which the authors speculatively link to the Big Bang, and which is built to avoid Boltzmann brains. This is the closest published relative of the owner's turn of a loop with a birth in it, and it comes with a stated measure problem (observers) attached. Read first, and read against Update 43 and O101.
2. **1606.08444**, Space from Hilbert Space: Recovering Geometry from Bulk Entanglement. ChunJun Cao, Carroll, Spyridon Michalakis. 2016. Phys. Rev. D 95, 024031 (2017). Pieces 1, 3. *Why:* builds a spatial manifold from the entanglement of "redundancy-constrained" states, mutual information as distance, multidimensional scaling to read off the dimension; perturbations of entanglement give local curvature obeying a spatial analog of Einstein's equation; the Hilbert space of a flat region is finite-dimensional and scales with volume, entropy with area. This is a graph-with-weighted-links picture of space with a dimension read off the wiring, the same move as the programme's dimension census, with entanglement where the programme has squares.
3. **1712.02803**, Bulk Entanglement Gravity without a Boundary: Towards Finding Einstein's Equation in Hilbert Space. ChunJun Cao, Carroll. 2017. Phys. Rev. D 97, 086003 (2018). Pieces 1, 3. *Why:* defines geometry from entanglement entropy across surfaces, Radon transforms to a metric, time evolution tracing out a four-dimensional spacetime, and an "entanglement equilibrium" argument that the geometry obeys Einstein's equation in the weak-field limit. The programme's piece 8 (a carrier for a pull) has no published derivation; this is one route from a graph-like state to gravity's equation and is the thing to compare the curve-first picture against.
4. **2005.12938**, Quantum Mereology: Factorizing Hilbert Space into Subsystems with Quasi-Classical Dynamics. Carroll, Ashmeet Singh. 2020. Phys. Rev. A 103, 022213 (2021). Pieces 1, 8. *Why:* an algorithm for splitting Hilbert space into system and environment from the Hamiltonian alone, by minimizing entanglement growth, with the remark that it bears on how spacetime emerges from entanglement. The owner's "which points are interchangeable is decided by the arrangement" (Update 12) is a factorization question in his language.
5. **2103.09780**, Reality as a Vector in Hilbert Space. Carroll. 2021. No journal given. Pieces 1, 8. *Why:* the stated program: the fundamental ontology is one vector evolving under the Schrödinger equation, the laws fixed by the Hamiltonian's spectrum alone, space and fields emergent. The programme's claim 3 (one underlying system, one relational quantity F) is a classical cousin; the comparison says what the programme would owe if it took a quantum version.
6. **hep-th/0410270**, Spontaneous Inflation and the Origin of the Arrow of Time. Carroll, Jennifer Chen. 2004. No journal given. Pieces 2, 7, 5. *Why:* a de Sitter vacuum with small positive vacuum energy is unstable to inflation at a higher scale, entropy increases without bound, and the universe is statistically time-symmetric on the largest scales with births in both time directions. This is a stable-for-now state giving birth, the shape of claim 4, but by fluctuation up rather than decay down; the difference is the thing to extract.
7. **0904.3115**, Dynamical compactification from de Sitter space. Carroll, Matthew C. Johnson, Lisa Randall. 2009. JHEP 0911:094. Pieces 2, 1. *Why:* D-dimensional de Sitter space is unstable to nucleating geometries with a different number of macroscopic dimensions, with the rate fastest for smaller cosmological constant, and an inflation model triggered by the compactification. This is a dimensional phase change by a seed, in continuum language, with the direction (large to small) opposite to the owner's uncurling; it belongs beside [W82] and [BV89] in the T12 reading and is the nearest published calculation of the rate the T54 barrier stands in for.
8. **1702.00850**, Why Boltzmann Brains Are Bad. Carroll. 2017. No journal given. Piece 5. *Why:* theories that predict Boltzmann brains are "cognitively unstable" (cannot be both true and justifiably believed), an argument that does not depend on choosing a measure. The owner's rule that chance-assembled brains are not real observers (Update 43) needs this paper as its benchmark, and rule 13 says it is a target, not a confirmation.
9. **1405.7577**, Self-Locating Uncertainty and the Origin of Probability in Everettian Quantum Mechanics. Charles T. Sebens, Carroll. 2014. Brit. J. Phil. Sci. 69(1), 25 to 74 (2018). Pieces 5, 8. *Why:* the Born rule as the uniquely rational credence under self-locating uncertainty, from one principle (changes to the environment alone do not change local probabilities), with the quantum Sleeping Beauty problem solved on the way. The owner's measure counts observers across turns of a loop; this is the published form of "where am I among the copies".

## Tier 2: read in part (the relevant sections only)

10. **0811.3772**, What if Time Really Exists? Carroll. 2008. Pieces 7, 2. *Why:* takes time as fundamental, the universe as a state obeying ordinary time-dependent quantum mechanics, and proposes a "Heraclitean universe" evolving forever in an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space as the source of the arrow. The programme's comparison-table position (an absolute ordering at the base, relativity's time emerging) is this position; read the section that states the assumption and what it costs.
11. **1801.08132**, Mad-Dog Everettianism: Quantum Mechanics at Its Most Minimal. Carroll, Ashmeet Singh. 2018. FQXi essay. Pieces 8, 1. *Why:* the spectrum of the Hamiltonian and one vector suffice; even a preferred algebra of observables is unnecessary because space and fields emerge. Short; read for the list of what has to be derived, which is also the programme's list.
12. **1704.00066**, The Hilbert Space of Quantum Gravity Is Locally Finite-Dimensional. Ning Bao, Carroll, Ashmeet Singh. 2017. Pieces 1, 3. *Why:* a region's density operator lives on a finite-dimensional factor; factors attach to regions only on decohered branches; quantum field theory is therefore not fundamental. The programme's finite graphs are finite by construction; this says a finite local state space is a position he argues for, not an approximation he apologizes for.
13. **1601.07558**, What is the Entropy in Entropic Gravity? Carroll, Grant N. Remmen. 2016. Phys. Rev. D 93, 124052. Piece 3. *Why:* separates holographic gravity from thermodynamic gravity and finds no self-consistent entropy for the latter. The programme's one candidate carrier without a new knob is entropic (O87, the warm-bath pull); this paper is the published objection to that route and must be read before the pull is run.
14. **1702.06959**, Quantum Circuit Cosmology: The Expansion of the Universe Since the First Qubit. Ning Bao, ChunJun Cao, Carroll, Liam McAllister. 2017. Pieces 1, 2. *Why:* expansion as a circuit entangling fresh ancilla qubits, at most about 140 e-folds for our Hubble volume, and no geometric description before the region was one Planck length across. A growing-count model of space, which the programme has parked (varying base-level count); read the setup.
15. **gr-qc/0505037**, Does Inflation Provide Natural Initial Conditions for the Universe? Carroll, Jennifer Chen. 2005. Gen. Rel. Grav. 37, 1671. Pieces 2, 7. *Why:* the short companion to hep-th/0410270: the natural state is nearly empty space, and inflation begins spontaneously there. Four pages; read whole if hep-th/0410270 is read.
16. **1108.0417**, Out of equilibrium: understanding cosmological evolution to lower-entropy states. Anthony Aguirre, Carroll, Matthew C. Johnson. 2011. Pieces 2, 7. *Why:* the most likely path of a fluctuation to lower entropy is the CPT conjugate of the most likely relaxation back, applied to stable and metastable de Sitter. This is the published arithmetic for "how does a stable-for-now state get over the wall by fluctuation", the question T38 and T54 ask of the toy.
17. **1505.02780**, Why Boltzmann Brains Don't Fluctuate Into Existence From the De Sitter Vacuum. Kimberly K. Boddy, Carroll, Jason Pollack. 2015. Piece 5. *Why:* in Many-Worlds with an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space, de Sitter settles into a stationary vacuum and no observers fluctuate out. Read beside 1702.00850 for the two halves of his position.
18. **2307.11927**, Completely Discretized, Finite Quantum Mechanics. Carroll. 2023. Pieces 1, 2. *Why:* a finite, discrete set of states; under simple spectral conditions the evolution is periodic, so discretized time visits finitely many vectors; the main challenges are cosmological. This is the parent of the 2026 cyclic paper and says why periodicity is on the table.
19. **2410.15468**, What Emergence Can Possibly Mean. Carroll, Achyuth Parola. 2024. Pieces 1, 8. *Why:* a classification of kinds of emergence by how wholes decompose into parts, with and without new ontological elements at higher levels. The programme says "space is a phase of X" and "energy exists only in the spacetime phase"; this gives the vocabulary for saying which kind of emergence that is.
20. **hep-th/0512148**, Is Our Universe Natural? Carroll. 2005. Nature review. Pieces 4, 5. *Why:* a survey of why the universe has the features it has, and of ideas that make them less surprising. Read the section on the arrow of time and the cosmological constant.
