# Bianconi's "gravity from entropy", and her discrete action: reading notes (5 October 2026)

The owner asked how the most recent work of researchers near this project interacts with it. **Read by an assistant
agent on 5 October 2026 from the arXiv HTML texts, searched locally; the owner has not read these passages.** Read
status is given per paper. Keys refer to `REFERENCES.bib`.

## In one paragraph

Gravity from entropy is a smooth, continuum theory of modified gravity. Its "entropy" is a relative entropy between two
metrics, a density at each point added up over the volume. It is not a count of what is hidden behind a surface, and
it is not a pull that needs a temperature. Nothing in it describes a sharp change between two arrangements, a lump of
energy released, leftovers, or a dimension appearing. So it neither contradicts this project's hypothesis nor gets
there first. Two pieces of it bear on this project: its auxiliary "G-field" is the nearest published relative of a
quantity living on the points (VISION Update 40); and her earlier discrete action on networks names changing the wiring
as open work.

## The papers

- **[Bia25] "Gravity from entropy"**, Phys. Rev. D 111, 066001 (2025); arXiv:2408.14391. *Read: Secs. I to IV in full,
  appendix A, parts of B and C.*
  - "The proposed entropic action is the quantum relative entropy between the metric of spacetime and the metric
    induced by the matter fields."
  - At low coupling the equations "reduce to the Einstein equations with zero cosmological constant".
  - An auxiliary field, the G-field, gives "an emergent small and positive cosmological constant only dependent on the
    G-field". No numerical value is given.
  - Dark matter: one sentence, "further research might clarify the role that the G-field could have for dark matter".
  - Stability is left open.
- **[Bia26] "Thermodynamics of the Gravity from Entropy theory"**, Phys. Rev. D 114, 024042 (2026); arXiv:2510.22545.
  *Read: the full main text.*
  - The entropy "differs conceptually from horizon entropies as it is local and volumeric and is not derived from
    horizons".
  - The dark-energy term is "dynamically generated by the G-field rather than imposed as a fundamental parameter".
  - The theory is reduced "to a single" parameter. It is "still in its infancy and needs to be validated and tested
    against cosmological and astrophysical observations".
- **"The quantum relative entropy of the Schwarzschild black-hole and the area law"**, Entropy 27, 266 (2025);
  arXiv:2501.09491. *Read: the full main text.* The entropy of a black hole follows the area law for a large hole,
  though it is computed as an integral over the inside; the constants differ from the thermodynamic ones.
- **[Bia24] "Quantum entropy couples matter with geometry"**, J. Phys. A 57, 365002 (2024); arXiv:2404.08556. *Read:
  Secs. 1 to 3, 4.3 to 4.8, 5.* **The discrete version**, on a complex of nodes, links and polygons.
  - It keeps the wiring fixed and says: "variation of the proposed action with respect to the topology could shed new
    light on the quest for emergent network geometry", citing Trugenberger's combinatorial quantum gravity among
    others.
  - Future work named: "geometrical phase transitions which could mimic black holes".
  - Its vacuum action depends on the wiring only through the spectra of the network's Laplacians (the agent's
    inference from its Eq. 41).
- **"The mass of simple and higher-order networks"**, J. Phys. A 57, 015001 (2024); arXiv:2309.07851. *Read: abstract,
  Secs. 1, 2.1, 2.2, conclusions.* A network "mass" from the spectrum and the count of independent cycles.
- **Network geometry with flavor; emergent hyperbolic network geometry** (with Rahmede; arXiv:1511.04539, 1607.05710).
  *Abstracts only.* Growing networks, not equilibrium ones.
- **By others, on her theory.** Inflation from the vacuum equations (Thattarampilly and Zheng, arXiv:2509.23987; *parts
  read*). Corrections to black holes and bounds from orbits near the galactic center (arXiv:2602.13694; *abstract and
  searched passages*). Two 2026 papers by D. S. Pereira reporting instabilities of the theory (arXiv:2607.18518,
  2609.03082; *abstract pages only*): "conventional Einstein normalization therefore implies a tachyonic spin-2
  instability".

## How it meets this project (*ours, from the agent's reading*)

- **The hidden count (T52).** Her route to gravity does not need a count that follows a surface. So if T52 finds that
  what a cut hides follows the volume, that closes Jacobson's and Verlinde's route in this model, and not hers.
- **A quantity on the points (Update 40).** Her G-field lives at every point and reduces to nothing in flat empty
  space. It is the closest published form of the ingredient the owner decided to add, and it comes with equations.
- **Dark energy first.** In her theory the dark-energy term is generated by the same field and matters most early, in
  the vacuum solutions others have studied. Loosely compatible with the owner's order; nothing is released.
- **A tool this project could use.** Her discrete vacuum action can be evaluated on any of this project's saved
  wirings from the Laplacian spectra, which the project already computes (O28). Whether flat, curled and leftover
  arrangements differ under it is an exact, cheap question, and it is the question she names as open.
- **A caution.** The theory's own stability is disputed in print this year.
