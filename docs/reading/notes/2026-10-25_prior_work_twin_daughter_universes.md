# Prior-work search: universes born in pairs, one with a matter surplus and one with the matching antimatter surplus, from a black hole

CLAUDE.md rule 15.

**The idea searched for** (the owner's, 25 October 2026, put in order): when a black hole "burps" a new universe, each
burp makes two daughter universes at once, identical except that one has a small matter surplus and the other the
matching antimatter surplus, so the parent black hole stays neutral and the total is net zero. Daughters of one mother
come in such pairs. A daughter's own daughters take the grandmother's sign, so the sign alternates by generation. In an
earlier form she said the mirror twin differs by the direction of the black hole's spin.

**The sentence it backs:** none written yet. This note says which parts of the idea are already in print, so that any
write-up cites them, and which parts we have not found.

## What came closest

Read status: "abstract" means the arXiv abstract page or a publisher or archive listing; "text" means the full text was
read, at least in part; "secondary" means only press or other papers' descriptions were seen.

**1. Robles-Pérez and collaborators: universe-antiuniverse pairs in third quantization.** Closest on "born as a pair,
matter in one and antimatter in the other".
- S. J. Robles-Pérez, "Restoration of matter-antimatter symmetry in the multiverse", arXiv:1706.06304 (2017); no
  journal reference found. **Read: text (ar5iv), in part.** Universes are created in entangled pairs, the expanding
  and contracting branches of the wave function of a closed de Sitter universe. The partner's time runs opposite
  (t_II = −t, after Linde's "antipodal" symmetry) and its matter field is the complex conjugate, so "matter is
  created, by definition, in the observer's universe and antimatter is always created in the partner universe." A
  Euclidean region between the two acts as a barrier. Only a charged scalar field is used: no fermions, no black
  hole, no parent spacetime beyond a remark about "a large parent universe like ours", no generations, nothing about
  spin.
- S. J. Robles-Pérez, "Quantum creation of a universe-antiuniverse pair", arXiv:2002.09863; Acta Phys. Pol. B Proc.
  Suppl. 13, 325 (2020), per the UC3M archive listing. **Read: abstract.** The same claim: pairs with reversed time
  flow, matter in one seen as antimatter from the other, so the matter-antimatter balance is restored across the
  multiverse.
- S. J. Robles-Pérez, "Quantum cosmology with third quantisation", arXiv:2110.05785; Universe (2021), per DOAJ.
  **Read: abstract.** A review whose main conclusion is that universes are most naturally created in entangled
  universe-antiuniverse pairs.
- Also seen, not read: Robles-Pérez, "Hartle-Hawking's vacuum is full of Vilenkin's universe-antiuniverse pairs",
  arXiv:2110.06521; "Time reversal symmetry in cosmology and the creation of a universe-antiuniverse pair",
  arXiv:1901.03387; Balcerzak, Barroso-Bellido, Dąbrowski and Robles-Pérez on entanglement between paired universes,
  arXiv:2009.14058 (abstract seen through a search summary only).
- **How close.** It shares the pair from one creation event and the matter-antimatter split across the pair, which is
  the core of her idea. It differs in three ways: the "anti" is defined by reversed time (CPT-like), where hers is two
  ordinary forward-running twins with opposite surpluses; the source is the vacuum of a quantized field on superspace,
  with no black hole as parent; and there are no generations, so no alternation by generation.

**2. L. Boyle, K. Finn and N. Turok, "CPT-Symmetric Universe", Phys. Rev. Lett. 121, 251301 (2018), arXiv:1803.08928.**
**Read: abstract.** The universe after the Big Bang is the CPT mirror image of the universe before it, together a
universe/anti-universe pair. The symmetry selects a vacuum, with consequences for the matter-antimatter asymmetry and
a right-handed-neutrino dark matter of about 4.8 × 10⁸ GeV; it predicts Majorana light neutrinos, a massless lightest
neutrino and no primordial long-wavelength gravitational waves. **How close:** a universe and an antiuniverse from one
Big Bang, but they are the two time-halves of one spacetime, not two daughters that run forward side by side; no black
hole parent; no generations. It is a mainstream, peer-reviewed relative and a natural citation.

**3. A. D. Sakharov, "Cosmological models of the universe with reversal of time's arrow", Zh. Eksp. Teor. Fiz. 79, 689
(1980); Sov. Phys. JETP 52, 349 (1980); revised in Usp. Fiz. Nauk (1991).** **Read: archive listing and secondary
descriptions only.** Per those, a hypothesis of cosmological CPT symmetry, with the arrow of time reversing at a point
of minimal entropy (or maximal expansion) in cyclic or many-sheeted models. Secondary sources (the Acta Phys. Pol. paper
above; Petit's papers) credit Sakharov with the idea that the matter excess here is balanced by an antimatter excess
across the reflection. **How close:** the ancestor of items 1 and 2; same differences. **To verify** by reading the
paper before it is cited for anything specific.

**4. N. J. Popławski: a universe inside every black hole (Einstein-Cartan torsion).** "Universe in a black hole with
spin and torsion", arXiv:1410.3881; Astrophys. J. 832, 96 (2016) per a search summary, not checked at the journal.
**Read: secondary.** Torsion from fermion spin stops collapse and each black hole forms a closed, nearly flat universe
beyond its horizon; in later talks and press he says the daughter inherits the parent black hole's spin axis as a
preferred direction (press and his slides only). Separately, "Matter-antimatter asymmetry and dark matter from
torsion", Phys. Rev. D 83, 084033 (2011), arXiv:1101.4012, **read: abstract**: torsion makes fermions and antifermions
decay differently, the antiparticles decay into hidden antimatter that forms dark matter, and total baryon number stays
zero, all inside one universe. **How close:** black hole as parent, the parent's spin carried by the daughter, and a
net-zero baryon number; but one daughter per black hole, and the antimatter balance is kept within that daughter (as
dark matter), not in a twin. The nearest published home for her black-hole-parent and spin-direction parts.

**5. L. Smolin, cosmological natural selection** (the "Did the universe evolve?" paper, Class. Quantum Grav. 9, 173
(1992), and *The Life of the Cosmos*, 1997; references from general knowledge, **not verified in this search**).
**Read: secondary.** Each black hole bounces into one daughter universe whose constants differ slightly from the
parent's, so universes that make many black holes dominate. **How close:** black hole parent and inheritance across
generations, the frame her alternation would sit in; no pairs, no matter-antimatter twins, no sign that alternates.

**6. A. D. Linde, "The universe multiplication and the cosmological constant problem", Phys. Lett. B 200, 272 (1988).**
**Read: secondary only** (descriptions in later papers). Two universes with their own metrics and fields, linked by an
"antipodal" symmetry that flips the sign of the action, so one carries negative energy and the vacuum energies cancel.
**How close:** a pair whose total is net zero, which is the shape of her bookkeeping; but the cancelled quantity is
energy, not baryon number, and there is no birth from a black hole.

**7. M. Gasperini (with Veneziano and others), pair production of universes from the string perturbative vacuum**, e.g.
"Birth of the Universe as anti-tunnelling", arXiv:gr-qc/0002080; Buonanno, Gasperini, Maggiore and Ungarelli,
arXiv:hep-th/9701146. **Read: secondary.** In third quantization the Wheeler-DeWitt wave function is amplified into
pairs of universes, one bouncing to the post-Big-Bang regime and one falling into the singularity. **How close:** pair
creation of universes, but the twins are not matter and antimatter and there is no black hole.

**8. Other relatives, further away.**
- S. M. Carroll and J. Chen, "Spontaneous inflation and the origin of the arrow of time", arXiv:hep-th/0410270 (2004).
  **Read: abstract.** Baby universes nucleate from de Sitter space both forward and backward in time, so the
  multiverse is statistically time-symmetric. Not matter against antimatter; not pairs from one parent in her sense.
- J. Barbour, T. Koslowski and F. Mercati, "Identification of a gravitational arrow of time", Phys. Rev. Lett. 113,
  181101 (2014), arXiv:1409.0917. **Read: secondary** (search summaries and Carlip's Physics viewpoint). A Newtonian
  N-body universe passes through a point of minimum size, the "Janus point", with arrows of time pointing away on both
  sides. Two halves of one history; nothing about antimatter or black holes.
- J.-P. Petit and G. d'Agostini, the Janus cosmological model (Mod. Phys. Lett. A, 2014; Astrophys. Space Sci., 1995,
  "Twin universes cosmology"). **Read: secondary.** One manifold with two metrics, a positive-mass and a negative-mass
  sector interacting only by gravity, presented as a version of Sakharov's twin universe in which our matter excess is
  balanced by an antimatter excess in the twin. Outside the mainstream: most sources are the authors' own site, and
  T. Damour wrote a critique (2019) of its field equations, whose conclusions were not read here.
- A 2021 paper contrasting fuzzballs and wormholes, arXiv:2111.05295, describes an evaporating black hole's interior
  detaching as a baby universe holding the infallen matter plus the negative-energy partners of Hawking pairs. **Read:
  search summary only.** One baby universe, negative energy rather than antimatter.

## Verdict

**PARTLY FOUND.**

- **Found:** universes created in pairs from one event, with the matter in one balanced by antimatter in the other, so
  the whole is symmetric (Robles-Pérez 2017 to 2021; Boyle, Finn and Turok 2018; Sakharov 1980 as the ancestor, to
  verify; Petit's twin-universe model, outside the mainstream). A pair whose total is net zero (Linde 1988, for
  energy). A black hole as the parent of a universe that inherits its spin axis (Popławski), and inheritance across
  generations of black-hole-born universes (Smolin).
- **Not found:** the combination of her three steps: (a) a black hole as the parent of the pair, so that the hole
  stays neutral; (b) two forward-running twins that differ only by the sign of a small surplus, rather than by reversed
  time (in the published pairs the "anti" partner is time-reversed, or the CPT image across the Big Bang); (c) the sign
  alternating by generation, a daughter's daughters taking the grandmother's sign. We have not found (b) as she means it
  or (c) at all, nor the spin-direction version of the twin.

So a write-up may say that pair creation of universe and antiuniverse is already published and cite Robles-Pérez and
Boyle, Finn and Turok; it may say "we have not found" only for the black-hole parent of a pair and the alternation by
generation, linking this note.
