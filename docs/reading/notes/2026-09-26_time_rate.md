## [Mag03] Magueijo, "New varying speed of light theories" (2003)

- **Is a varying c meaningful?** Only dimensionless numbers are measured, but the same objection applies to e, ħ, G;
  if the fine-structure constant varies "something ... has to be varying", and the choice of which is the one that makes
  the dynamics simplest (Sec. 2). Two cases are dimensionless outright: a colour-dependent c, and in bimetric theories
  "the ratio between the speed of the photon and that of the graviton" (Sec. 2.5).
- **Gravity and light at different speeds: bimetric theories** (Sec. 3.2; Clayton and Moffat, Drummond). Two metrics,
  one for gravity and one for matter; "special relativity is still realized within each sector"; usually the graviton's
  speed differs from light's. Used for inflation without an inflaton, for dark matter (Drummond: gravity stronger
  beyond a galactic length scale) and for dark energy. A structure-formation scenario has the speed of gravity
  "vanishingly small in the very early universe" (Sec. 5.1).
- **Preferred frames.** Hard breaking puts "a preferred frame in physics (usually identified with the cosmological
  frame)" and breaks time-translation invariance, so energy is not conserved (Secs. 3.1, 4.3); its effects on local
  physics must be "subtle". Moffat's version keeps exact local Lorentz symmetry in the laws and breaks it in the vacuum,
  a timelike field that picks a frame and, he argues, the arrow of time (Sec. 3.4).
- **A first-order change of c.** Moffat's model has c undergo "a first or second order phase transition" to a value 30
  orders of magnitude smaller (Sec. 3.4); a cyclic picture of de Sitter space with sporadic Big Bang "events" triggered
  by a minimal perturbation is called untestable "philosophy" (pp. 24-25). No front or nucleation treatment.
- **Acceleration and mass.** Not in the paper as a mechanism; inertial forces are named a probe of frame-dependent
  versions (p. 20).
- **Constraints** (as of 2003): Oklo and geology, the fine-structure redshift claim, CMB, cosmic rays; "tight, but not
  insurmountable". Predates the 2017 neutron-star merger.

## [Ell14] Ellis, "The evolving block universe and the meshing together of times" (2014)

- "The past has been determined and is fixed"; "The future is uncertain and still has to be fixed"; the present "is
  the future boundary of the determinate spacetime region" and "continually moves to the future" (Sec. 2).
- **Preferred time:** built from matter (Ricci eigenlines, proper time along them; cosmic time in an FLRW universe). He
  states "my program specifically introduces a preferred frame" and "I reject physical realism about Minkowski
  spacetime"; simultaneity "is a purely psychological construct" (Secs. 1.1, 2.2, 7.2).
- **Rate:** "Time flows at the rate of one second per second, as determined by the metric tensor locally at each
  event"; local rates differ with gravitational potential; "The global rate derives from the combination of these
  local rates" (Obj. 2).
- **Two times:** global time (set by large masses) and local time (experienced); "Time dilation takes place between
  universal time (scale 3) and local times (scale 2)"; local time "cannot give greater times than global time" (Sec. 4).
- **Not in the paper:** Lorentz invariance, whether the preferred frame is detectable, the speed of gravity or light.

## [ER10] Ellis and Rothman, "Time and spacetime: the crystallizing block universe" (2010)

- The present is where quantum collapse turns possibility into fact, event by event along world lines; "not all
  features become fixed at the same time"; a "main resolution front" with "pockets of residue remaining behind for a
  time" (Sec. 6, Fig. 3). "The arrow of time arises simply because the future does not yet exist" (p. 21).
- **Not in the paper:** any rate for the front, relativity of simultaneity, Lorentz invariance, clocks.

## Against the owner's band (Ours)

- **Closest published match:** Ellis's evolving block universe: past fixed, future open, a moving present with a
  preferred frame set by matter, advancing locally at the proper-time rate, with dilation as differences between
  global and local time. He takes the same step the owner did (a real present, relativity emergent) and pays the same
  price (a preferred frame whose undetectability he does not address).
- **What the owner's picture adds:** a mechanism at the front (uncurling, energy released) and a floor behind it (time
  re-curled). **What it lacks that his has:** a rule for the rate; his is proper time, one second per second.
- **Gravity against light:** bimetric theories are the published form; since 2017 the neutron-star merger bounds the
  difference today to about one part in 10^15 (general knowledge, to verify), so different mechanisms are allowed only
  if their speeds agree now; early-universe differences remain possible in principle.
