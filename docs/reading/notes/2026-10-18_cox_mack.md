## Katie Mack

- **Research** [search summary]: dark matter and the early universe, read through its effect on the earliest cosmic
  structures. Recent papers named by her Perimeter profile: "Cosmology of single species hidden dark matter" (Journal of
  Cosmology and Astroparticle Physics, 2025) and, with Hou, dark matter annihilation and dark-matter–baryon velocity
  offsets at Cosmic Dawn (2025). A citation index lists 36 works as of February 2026; no 2026 paper was confirmed.
- **Books** [search summary]: *The End of Everything (Astrophysically Speaking)* (2020); *How to Build a Universe
  (Theoretically Speaking)* announced for early 2027.
- **Bearing on the toy, three places, each a benchmark and not a confirmation:**
  1. **Hidden-sector dark matter** is the published family VISION Update 34's "a charge per kind" belongs to (dark
     photons, mirror matter; `docs/reading/notes/2026-09-26_dark_charge.md`). Her 2025 hidden-dark-matter paper would be
     a current entry point for what observations already limit. Not read.
  2. **Dark matter and the first structures** is where the owner's idea of today (the scraps as the seeds of structure;
     `sandbox/NOTEBOOK.md`, 18 October) would be judged: what the earliest structures need of dark matter's clustering.
     The toy's scraps are knots of fixed energy whose number is set at the opening (O107); a sprinkle of them at random
     is shot noise, which is the thing to check against before anything is claimed.
  3. **Vacuum decay**, a chapter of *The End of Everything*, is the standard form of "spacetime is metastable and could
     convert" (VISION Update 10; [C77]). It is the clearest popular account of the picture claim 4 borrows, and it says
     what the standard calculation needs (a lower vacuum, a bubble, a wall speed) that the toy's layer form does not have
     (no front; Update 65). Not read.
