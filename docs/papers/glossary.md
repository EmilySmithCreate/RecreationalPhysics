## Not used, and why

- **Spinodal** for the edge near λ = 1.37. At g = 1.5 the torus stops being stuck there, but its barriers
  are still 8 to 10 units, so the edge is where the wait drops below the 200-sweep watch, not where the
  barrier vanishes. The word would claim more than the data shows.
- **Allotrope** for the relic. [T24]'s allotropes are regions of any size stuck in a different discrete
  arrangement, left behind as his coupling runs, at λ = 1. The relic is a single object of fixed size, made
  by the burp, with a measured count depending on protocol and seed number, at λ > 1: a grain of unreacted X, not a second product. Cousins, not the same
  thing.
- **Topological defect** for the relic. Nothing here shows it is topologically protected; T19 shows it can
  heal.
- **Universe**, in claims. The owner's word is **reality**, which is wider; "universe" implies spacetime.
