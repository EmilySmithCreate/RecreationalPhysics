## The claim, in the author's words

Our spacetime is one settled arrangement of something deeper, X: a specific, relatively stable arrangement with its own dimensions, curled where ours are open. X was stable for now, not for ever. Most likely X was a slab, two directions already open and one still curled tight; each curled direction stands behind its own wall and releases its own share when it opens. Energy arriving in X, as a black hole's infall arrives, raised its excitation until, in one small part, the curled direction gave way; the change spread as a front, X opened into space, and the change came out as a burp: a fixed release of energy, which became the Big Bang's energy. Dark energy is the energy of space itself; where it comes from is open. What did not convert remained as scrap: small knots of fixed energy, their number set at the opening; the scrap is the best current guess for the dark matter, and whether it is the dark matter is open. What the burp does not deliver returns to the black hole, ready to start another burp. Energy was conserved across the change. The rules of the two phases are their constants and do not change from one generation to the next. Inside the space that forms, black holes are regions where the parent's own geometry re-curls, two directions still open and one curled tight; what falls in raises the excitation, and the chance of a new opening rises with it. A daughter space may open while the black hole remains in the parent; each universe ends when its last black hole has dissolved, and every black hole births many new ones, so the tree of universes multiplies each generation. Every possible arrangement of what a Big Bang produces therefore gets to exist. The quantum world is the set of undetectably different versions of an arrangement, all standing at once, until an interaction makes them distinguishable; counting those versions alone is not enough: the rule that lets them cancel is the published one, versions carry amplitudes and a record in the surroundings is what keeps them apart, and where the loop supplies it is open. *(Her current best guess. The claim of 9 October (VISION Update 50) was confirmed by her; this version carries her decisions since: the tie withdrawn and the directions curling separately, each behind its own wall (Update 58, 16 October); a feed rather than a spark (rule 11's fifth use, 16 October); the black hole as the parent's own geometry curled, infall raising the excitation, a daughter without a whole-universe reset and the branching count (Update 59, both decisions confirmed by her the same evening); the published amplitude rule adopted (Update 57, 15 October). The sentences that changed are ours in wording and hers once she confirms them; until then the text of 9 October, in `VISION.md` Update 50, is the confirmed one.)*

## The 5 October reading, and what it changes

Six sets of papers were read on 4 and 5 October, by assistant agents from the arXiv text and in two cases from PDFs
the author supplied; the author has not read the passages; the notes, with the read status of every source, are in
`docs/reading/notes/2026-10-05_*.md`. *Ours throughout.*

- **The model's author's programme** ([T22] to [T25], parts of [KTB19]; O96). The factor of two that separated our
  three-direction curve from his published one is in his own Eq. (1), which counts every link from both ends. What still
  differs (our hot tail lower, our plateau higher) is in the direction that graphs allowing triangles and pentagons
  would explain; his text never says "bipartite". So the question for him narrows to two facts, the size of that figure's
  graphs and whether they were two-sided. His current position on the order of his transition is a hybrid, two
  continuous branches with a jump (his email of 22 September, private). Paper 4 of the series, the order of the
  transition in three directions at matched sizes with replica exchange, is the one bounded joint project worth
  offering him.
- **Bianconi's gravity from entropy** ([Bia24], [Bia25], [Bia26]; O97, O102). A continuum theory whose action is a
  relative entropy between two metrics; local and volumetric, not horizons, so not Jacobson's or Verlinde's count and
  not a pull that needs warmth. It has no sharp change between two arrangements, no release, no leftovers, no dimension
  appearing: it neither contradicts the hypothesis nor gets there first. Two things bear on this project. Her G-field, a
  quantity at every point that reduces to nothing in flat empty space, is the closest published form of the quantity on
  the points the author decided to add for gravity (Update 40). And her discrete action, which she keeps at fixed wiring
  and names varying the wiring as open, was evaluated on our wirings (exploratory, O102): under one of her two forms flat
  space is the lowest, each curled direction costs about the same, and a scrap costs a little, which is the shape of this
  project's ladder at λ > 1; under the other the order reverses. One narrow question for her: which form is meant when the
  wiring varies, and at which metric.
- **Group field theory condensates, and dynamical triangulations** ([MOPT23], [Ori21], [AGGN22], [AL26]; O98).
  Neither has an order-to-order change with a change of dimension and a release. Group field theory takes its one
  transition as continuous by the method chosen, makes the Big Bang a bounce inside the geometric phase, and its author
  writes that no notion of time applies across the phases, so a reader from it will ask what clock the tube opens
  against (the fair clock is this project's answer). Triangulations hold a caution for T6, two of their transitions were
  first read as higher order and settled only at tens of thousands of building blocks, and the nearest published support
  for T7: [AGGN22] conjectures that changes requiring a rearrangement of the whole configuration are first order, which
  is the kind of change a tube opening into a sheet is, though theirs is a statement about equilibrium ensembles.
- **Disordered locality, quantum graphity, and dimensions that open in order** ([MS07], [PWS09], [QSMG12], [H13],
  [St14]; O99). The far-reaching links found among the leftovers of spaces that opened from several seeds (O95, a first
  look) have a published cousin: in [QSMG12] domains that nucleate separately need not merge and the boundary between
  them is frozen in by the quench; same cause, a different object, and no count or scaling was found in what was read.
  They are not dark energy, by [PWS09]'s own derivation, which needs their number to grow with the volume and their
  ends to be uncorrelated; links frozen once are fixed in number and short. [H13] bounds such defects. [St14] has
  dimensions opening one after another as the universe cools, as a crossover; set beside an opening with a fixed push
  and an exact release it is paper 8.
- **The loop, counting per turn, and comparing infinities** ([Freivogel11], [BHKP07], [Lehners12], [Bostrom11],
  [Askell18]; O101). The author's count per turn has a standard form, the long-run average per period (the
  renewal-reward theorem), with three conditions: turns alike (her rule that the constants do not change supplies it);
  a finite yield per turn (her "our stable phase never ends" would make one turn infinite, so a turn needs a stated end,
  such as the last star); and a common unit between loops (per turn drops how long a turn lasts). A trap: if one turn
  makes many black holes the process branches and the rate of multiplying decides what dominates. Her answer of 5 October
  (Update 43): maximize both, with the reward being observers who evolved, and black holes too close together cut the
  cold time short, so the two are tied. Her decision of 16 October (Update 59): the count is the branching one, each
  universe ending when its last black hole has dissolved and every black hole birthing many new ones (piece 12). The one published maximizing principle, [BHKP07], counts the entropy produced,
  which is dominated by starlight warming dust; requiring observers to have evolved is a published answer to the
  chance-brain problem ([Freivogel11] reviews it).
- **Matter and antimatter** ([KLZ09], [PV13], [MRM12], abstracts). In standard physics ordinary matter is the small
  excess left when matter and antimatter annihilated; asymmetric dark matter holds that dark matter is the same kind of
  leftover excess, which would make 5.36 a ratio of two leftovers, as the author guessed, and not of two releases; and
  the best-studied way to make such an excess is at the walls of the bubbles of a first-order transition, so the burp's
  front is where an excess could be made. Nothing in the toy comes in opposite kinds; this is a direction, not a result.

## Where each piece stands

The tests use one published model of emergent geometry (Trugenberger's combinatorial quantum gravity) as a
toy: space is a network, X's stand-in is a torus with one direction curled (spherical cows being unavailable),
and the model's local-term coefficient λ is a knob. Only λ = 1 is his model; the torus results are at λ > 1,
where the energy is no longer a curvature, so they are about a neighbour of his model, and are never called
combinatorial quantum gravity. Anything about gravity is tested in three dimensions only: in two, even
Einstein's gravity gives no pull between masses at rest (general knowledge, to verify), so no number measured
in 2D is carried into a claim about gravity. **A toy model can show that a mechanism is possible or
impossible inside a class of models; on its own it cannot show that reality works this way.** That is the
author's claim, and the model is how she tests its pieces.
