## 1. The question we are exploring

The focus is **primordial density perturbations**—the tiny spatial variations associated with the later cosmic structure—not the toy simulation's *seeds that initiate uncurling*. We also discussed a possible origin of the **matter–antimatter asymmetry**, but the author explicitly wants that treated as a separate research topic, not solved within the density-perturbation investigation.

The author's new candidate is that **additional energy continues to flow into the black-hole-associated curled region during the short interval in which its opening transition is underway**. That late contribution may be tiny compared with the larger stored energy released at opening. The author is interested in whether slightly asynchronous entry and conversion could leave spatial variations in the daughter's initial energy or later matter distribution.

**Important refinement:** The author specifically pictures the late inflow as **spread throughout the curled structure**, not injected through a single doorway or location. Its spatial distribution and correlations have not been specified; "spread throughout" does **not** entail either perfect uniformity or useful perturbations.

## 2. The author's selected sequence (confirmed in conversation)

1. **Black-hole-associated curling:** One spatial direction in a connected relational arrangement is curled/compact inside a black-hole-associated region. The exact geometry and parent–daughter map remain conjectural.
2. **Accumulation:** Incoming parent-universe matter/energy is converted into energy stored in the curled region. Incoming matter does **not** retain its particle identity.
3. **Threshold:** Stores accumulate until the opening threshold is reached. The user has previously specified that **lambda is a universal model coefficient, not something that rises with energy**. Do not make stored energy alter lambda.
4. **Overlap interval:** The opening transition begins, and **further energy continues arriving while the transition is still in progress**. The region is envisioned as opening almost all at once, but the author allows a brief transition interval.
5. **Completion before particle production:** The author chose that **the entire curled region opens first**, then particle creation and annihilation occur inside the daughter space. Do not impose particle reactions at partially opened sites as a premise.
6. **Finite daughter space, subsequent expansion:** The initial opening produces an existing amount of space. Subsequent expansion is pictured as **stretching existing relationships**, not additional spatial regions continually uncurling. A consistent dynamical metric is not yet formulated.
7. **Particle era:** Energy reappears as particles. Matter–antimatter pairs can be produced and annihilate; annihilation produces radiation. The hot plasma can also recreate pairs while temperatures permit. There is no single instantaneous final flash; early-universe radiation evolves and thermalizes.
8. **Late contribution:** A **very small additional energy inflow** during the opening could, in the author's proposal, survive into the daughter as extra particle-forming energy and might imprint subtle spatial differences. The author mentioned a **roughly one-in-a-billion** scale in connection with matter–antimatter asymmetry, but this was a conceptual suggestion, **not** a calculated energy fraction or prediction for primordial density-contrast amplitude.

## 3. Two separate questions — do not conflate them

### A. 'Room' in the existing sealed toy (already measured)

The submitted draft paper *The rule, the room, and which settings give a birth* defines **room** as the number **C of nonnegative-energy stores** available to absorb energy released from the graph during a sealed conversion. This is **not** physical volume, heat capacity, or expansion. Its toy findings include:

- At lambda = 1.25 in the T9 runs, few stores favored melting/disorder; enough stores favored a sheet-like endpoint.
- At N = 96 in T18, the smallest store counts yielding a majority of 'births' were **12, 36, and 72** for lambda **1.10, 1.25, and 1.40**. The threshold increased with release; this was tested at only one size.
- The saved zero-energy end states usually become a flat torus of **minimal width six**, rather than a macroscopic growth of the short direction. **Graph rearrangement into a locally flat configuration is not yet physical spatial expansion.**

These are observations of the specified Markov-chain model, not evidence that real energy is confined by an early-universe volume. The user's proposed late influx is a **different mechanism**.

### B. Space first, then hot particles (current conceptual thread)

In the author's proposed physical narrative, the whole region opens and then energetic particles are produced. Existing relationships subsequently stretch. The question is whether energy entering *during the transition* leaves distinguishable differences in the initial post-opening energy field that persist, rather than being completely homogenized.

This is **not** equivalent to ordinary matter–antimatter annihilation. Annihilation of initially equal populations does not, by itself, explain why a net baryon excess survives. Explaining the tiny baryon excess requires a mechanism with the relevant symmetry breaking and nonequilibrium physics (Sakharov conditions or an equivalent account). The author requests **parking this** while investigating density structure.

## 4. What the earlier exchange established about primordial density perturbations

- The simplest toy has a **uniform release per converting point**: a smooth burp by construction. Knots, relics, and boundaries produced *behind* an opening front do not automatically account for the observed primordial perturbation field.
- Opening at multiple starting locations (e.g., discussed T37/T53) creates timing and seed-spacing structure, but this is not the same thing as an observed nearly scale-invariant spectrum. The toy has no physical cosmological expansion rule to stretch such patterns to cosmological distances.
- Random local defects tend to give the wrong correlations. Defect-dominated cosmologies also have difficulty reproducing the observed coherent acoustic-peak pattern because they continuously source disturbances rather than setting coherent initial conditions.
- The **horizon/correlation problem** remains: a local finite-speed mechanism cannot simply impose correlations across domains that had no causal contact in the daughter history. A model might resolve this with a prior correlated substrate, a different causal structure, or a demonstrable stretching history; none has been shown for Omega.
- For reference, the observed primordial perturbations are approximately Gaussian, predominantly adiabatic, nearly scale-invariant with scalar spectral index near **0.965**, and yield coherent CMB acoustic peaks. These are quantitative targets, not optional aesthetic criteria.

## 5. Key conceptual caution

**If the curled starting state, late inflow, transition law, and resulting expansion are exactly spatially uniform and deterministic, their outputs remain spatially uniform.** Large overall pressure, rapid expansion, particle annihilation, or high energy density alone do not create an inhomogeneous field out of exact symmetry.

A viable mechanism must identify an actual symmetry-breaking input or instability: for example, microscopic fluctuations, spatially uneven incoming energy, stochastic opening-time differences, or fluctuations in geometry/relationships. These are *candidates to test*, not yet accepted additions to the author's model. Crucially, **the author has not answered whether stretching is uniform or has built-in unevenness**; this is the outstanding question at the end of the conversation.

Also distinguish three quantities that can easily be confused:

- **Relative baryon excess:** roughly one surviving baryon per order-of-magnitude billion early particle–antiparticle pairs (temperature-dependent shorthand; precision requires baryon-to-photon ratio and entropy accounting).
- **Relative primordial density perturbation:** often characterized at order 10^-5 in CMB temperature anisotropies, with curvature amplitude measured via a power spectrum; this is **not the same** as the baryon asymmetry.
- **Late incoming energy fraction:** presently undefined; an illustrative one-in-a-billion inflow cannot be assumed to generate either observational quantity automatically.

## 7. Minimal falsifiable simulation program (without fitting the sky yet)

**Stage 0 — audit the current toy.** Preserve its current Hamiltonian and its constant lambda; retain past T9/T18/T37/T53/T60 results and their scope. Identify what the current graph stores, chain sweeps, conversion signatures, and endpoint coordinates actually mean. The chain's Monte Carlo steps are **not established physical time**. Keep the fixed-C sealed reservoir experiment separate from a proposed parent-energy influx.

**Stage 1 — symmetry control.** Implement or analytically assess a spatially homogeneous inflow coupled during the transition, with homogeneous conversion and a deterministic expansion rule. Predict a **zero spatial perturbation** if all inputs and laws are exactly symmetric. This guards against attributing a numerical artifact to physics.

**Stage 2 — measure spatial response, not just total release.** For an explicitly specified minimal influx schedule and conserved energy ledger, record incoming energy by emergent neighborhood, transition-completion order, post-opening energy per emergent volume, and eventual spatial differences. Define fluctuations, e.g. delta(x) = [rho(x)-mean(rho)]/mean(rho), only after a meaningful emergent distance/volume measure is established. Make sure late influx is not double-counted as both already-stored and newly released energy.

**Stage 3 — compare controls.** Hold total late inflow fixed and compare (i) exact uniform deposition, (ii) time-varying but still spatially uniform deposition, and (iii) spatially variable deposition whose variation is justified independently. If only (iii) gives density contrasts, the mechanism has **transmitted** a preexisting variation, not spontaneously explained its origin. Do not treat an arbitrary random pattern as a discovery.

**Stage 4 — stretching and survival.** Specify what it means for relationships to stretch and how density/redshift evolves with it; test whether the deposited contrasts persist, wash out through mixing/thermalization, or become unstable. The current graph toy **does not yet demonstrate a cosmological scale factor or causal light cones**, so this may require a separate effective model before any CMB comparison.

**Stage 5 — cosmology gating criteria.** Only after a physically interpretable evolution exists, compute spatial two-point correlations and a power spectrum. Ask whether the resulting perturbations have the observed amplitude/tilt, coherence, Gaussianity, adiabatic character, superhorizon relationships, and acoustic peaks **without fitting away failures using new knobs**. A simple broad-band power law is not by itself sufficient.

**Stage 6 — keep baryogenesis separate.** If and when the author returns to the asymmetry idea, specify what quantum numbers or charges the parent transfer carries or generates, what creates an imbalance in reaction rates, and how matter number emerges despite lost incoming particle identities. Track energy conservation and baryon number separately. Do not label a one-in-a-billion *energy* surplus as a solution to baryogenesis.
