# The whiteboard picture of 15 October, read against the record

The owner drew her picture of the whole cycle on a whiteboard on 15 October 2026 (three photos in
`docs/figures/whiteboard_2026-10-15/`) and described it in words (`sandbox/NOTEBOOK.md`, 15 October, evening).
She asked three things: is the mathematics already aligned with the picture, can it be aligned, and has anything
measured made the picture impossible or very unlikely. This page answers element by element. Every status word
below is one of the record's kinds (exact, measured, interpretation, the hypothesis's claim), and every line
points at the entry that holds the fact (CLAUDE.md rule 17). Nothing here is a new result.

**The short answer.** The picture is in three parts. The first part, one slice of space being born from a ring
four around, is already what the record says, number for number: the spark of 12, the release of 1 per point,
the scrap, and a product six around. The second part, space as a stack of such slices with one relic in each,
agrees with the toy in shape and fails in one exact fact: a relic dropped into a stacked slice is not a stuck
arrangement any more, and no stuck relic has been found in three-direction space at all. The third part, the
black hole curling one direction of space under concentrated energy, has been tried eight ways and nothing has
curled; it now rests on the ingredient she adopted this morning, curling energy, whose form is not yet written.
Nothing measured makes the picture impossible. Two exact facts make it harder than it looks, and both are cheap
to test further.

## What is aligned, what is not, what is open

**Aligned already, and exactly.** The slice. Her numbers are paper 1's numbers, and her "height 4 becomes height
6" is the correction of 11 October (O116) in her own picture: the product is a ring six round, not a sheet.
Her tie's bookkeeping is right too: under "all at the last" the fold's first direction takes everything and the
burp's one opening gives everything back (O106), which is her "all the energy goes to curling the first
direction" and "the 3 pop together". And the cycle closes on itself in her picture as it does in the toy's
ladder: space at height 6, the black hole's inside at height 4 (her slab, the programme page's X), the burp
back to 6. In two directions the second half of that cycle, 4 to 6, is measured many thousands of times.

**Aligned in shape, not in one fact: the relic in a stacked slice.** A stack of slices is the six-link torus,
so the drawing is the right drawing. But the relic she puts in each slice is a two-direction object, and the
record has already priced what happens to it when the slice is given neighbours: it is no longer a dip
(O56), and no stuck curled object has been found in six-link flat space at all (O58, searched at λ = 1.02 over
one- and two-switch constructions). So "one relic in each slice, carrying curling energy, looking like dark
matter" rests, in the toy, on an object that does not yet exist. *Narrowed the same evening by a sandbox look (`sandbox/LOG.md`, 15 October; not promoted): the sentence holds for flat six-link space; in the slab product of the rod's opening, one direction still curled, stuck curled pieces of 1 to 14 points were found in 48 saved end states. Whether any of them is a dip in flat space is still the listing above.* This is the single fact most worth her
attention, because her dark-matter half and her black-hole seed both stand on it (NOTEBOOK 14 October: the
black hole "connects open space to a relic").

**Not seen, eight times: energy curling flat space.** T21, T26, T27, T34, T47 part B, T57, and the two sandbox
looks of 14 and 15 October. Ordinary energy breaks squares (16 units at once, thousands of ways) before it
curls points (4(λ − 1) each, with no single move out of flat space that adds a square); energy that may only
curl buys separate columns and does not grow a region. The notebook of 15 October already reads her fold as
the burp run backwards; the record's answer so far is that it does not run backwards on its own. What could
change that is the design of curling energy (Update 57), from her ten answers.

**Outside the toy, and not contradicted.** Gravity as the cause of the knot, dark energy as the loop's vacuum,
the measure, and "everything everywhere all at once". Each is the hypothesis's claim or an adopted rule; the
toy neither supports nor counts against them, and the page says so where they appear.

## Why the picture has been hard to explain (ours)

Three words have carried two meanings each, and the drawing uses each in one sense while the record uses it
in the other.

- **Wide.** In the drawing, height 6 is "wide". In the record, six round is the narrowest flat torus, and
  "wide" has meant wider than six (T61, T62, T64). Her picture needs only six. If that is what she means, the
  widening question (T62, T64) is a different question from hers, and paper 1's product is exactly her slice.
- **Relic.** In two directions it is a stuck column of fixed energy. In three directions the same word has
  been used for an object that has not been found; the record says "not found", the drawing says "one in each
  slice".
- **Knot.** The programme page's knots are the scrap (O107: eight-point objects and double columns in a
  two-direction sheet). The drawing's knot is a fully curled region of three-direction space, which is the
  6-cube (O41). Both are stuck arrangements; they are not the same object.

## The cheapest tests, exact and with no run

1. **Is a relic in a stacked slice ever a dip?** Build 6 × L × W by stacking, plant each of the three scraps of
   O107 (the 14-unit column, the knot of two kinds, the double column) in one slice, and list every single move
   out, at λ = 1.02, 1.25 and 1.40. O56 did this for the one-switch column at 1.02 and found it falls apart;
   the other two scraps and the higher curling costs are not listed. If none is a dip at any λ, the dark-matter
   half of the picture needs a different carrier in three directions.
2. **Three-switch constructions, and the end states of T34 and T57 quenched to their nearest dip**, which is
   how the two-direction relic was found (O13, O16; named in O58 as what remains).
3. **The smallest curled patch in flat six-link space against the same number of broken points**, under the
   table and under one that charges damage (TASKS, 11 October, item 4; O114). This prices the door her black
   hole has to go through against the door the energy takes instead.

Each is an afternoon of exact listing. A registered run on the fold waits for the design brief of curling
energy (Update 57) and for her answers to the ten questions of 15 October.
