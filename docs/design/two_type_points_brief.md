# Two types of point: the design brief (draft, 20 October 2026)

*Written from the author's decision of 19 October, late (VISION Update 74; `sandbox/NOTEBOOK.md`, the same night). Under
VISION S1 the form below is fixed before any registered run; nothing is built until she answers the questions at the end.
The model with it is our family around the published one, never combinatorial quantum gravity. Each item says whose it is.*

## What she decided, in her words

"Yes make them diff types that repulse but if they touch they neutralize and the links on them connect with the closest
free one of opposite type." With, the same night: "what if one point is a curling type and the other an uncurling
point"; "the extra square costs because it forces a less than optimal setup of the dot arrangement ... some repulse on
being too close to too much of your own dot type. As it curls it also gets too close to its own dot type, but then the 2
types of points become one and neutralize"; and "they release curling energy when they heal as connecting to a not close
point stretches the link then it snaps back (curl)".

## What the toy already has (exact)

1. **Two kinds of point, and every link joins one of each.** The toy is two-sided; the two kinds are equal in number by
   the degree rule, and its energy treats them exactly alike.
2. **Same-kind repulsion.** A square is two points of one kind sharing two neighbours of the other, so the square count is
   the number of close same-kind pairs. The hard-core rule forbids any same-kind pair from sharing more than two
   neighbours (Q2), and the curling cost charges each link for squares beyond two: crowding costs, and too much crowding is
   forbidden.
3. **Neutralizing in pairs.** In the layer form (Updates 63, 65) points vanish in linked pairs, one of each kind, and their
   neighbours join up. Done one pair at a time it was tried exhaustively on 18 October (`sandbox/pair_moves.py`, a look):
   removing one linked pair from flat 6 × 6 × 6, only 5 of the 120 ways of rejoining the ten loose links are valid, and
   every one leaves 24 to 26 points broken; from the slab none is valid. Done for a whole layer at once it is clean and
   gives the slab exactly. Her rule "connect with the closest free one of opposite type" is one of the 120 ways, so as a
   single-pair move it is already known to break points; as a whole-layer move it is the layer form.

## What is new

The two kinds made different. Today nothing in the energy tells a curling point from an uncurling one, so swapping the two
kinds changes nothing. Making them differ is the new ingredient, and it is the one that could give two opposite kinds that
are not mirror images, which dark matter against dark antimatter needs (notebook, 19 October).

## Questions for her (the brief cannot be finished without them)

1. **What makes a curling point different from an uncurling one in the energy?** (a) Curling energy is held only at
   curling points: a curled link's extra cost is paid from, and released to, the store of its curling end; (b) the two
   kinds pay different curling costs; (c) something else, in her words.
2. **What counts as touching?** (a) a linked pair on a link at the tightest curl (three squares, the most the hard-core
   rule allows); (b) a whole layer of such links at once, which is the layer form's compaction; (c) something else.
3. **The rejoining rule.** "The closest free one of opposite type": closest by steps on the graph before the pair is
   removed, ties broken in a fixed order, the next closest taken if the closest would break the hard-core rule. Is that
   it? And, given item 3 above, is a single pair allowed to neutralize, at the cost of the broken points it leaves, or only
   a whole layer?
4. **Where the released curling energy goes when a knot heals** ("it snaps back"): into the stores of the points that
   healed, or shared with the neighbourhood?

## What a first registered run would read (after her answers)

Whether, with the two kinds made different, a sealed opening leaves the two kinds' share of curling energy unequal, and
whether the knots it leaves come in two kinds in unequal numbers: the toy's version of a net dark matter over dark
antimatter. Registered with her prediction before it runs.
