## 3. The question, as it would be registered

In a six-link slab 4 × L × L (one direction curled to four, two open), untied, named points, the single-switch chain,
opened from k planted seeds in a bath at a fixed coupling until its curled rung is almost gone, and then cooled on the
fair clock as T51 cooled the two-direction sheet: at the end of the cold hold, read from the saved wiring, with the
seed's own flaws tagged by position at sweep 0 and scored apart,

1. **Rings.** Are there closed curled rings inside the open space that the front made, and are they stuck? Two objects,
   both counted, each conditional on stage 0 finding that it can exist (section 9, items 5 and 6):
   (1a) the **remnant column**, four points at the slab's own rung (d = 2) forming a square whose four links carry five
   squares, every neighbor outside it at d = 3: a piece of the mother left in the daughter, paper 2's column in kind;
   (1b) the **open ring** of the 23 October look, a square whose four links carry five squares with all four points at
   d = 3. Each counts only if it is a dip (every single move touching it costs energy at the run's λ).
2. **The lip.** Where do the links below four squares (the uncurl) that the front made sit: inside clean open space
   (both ends at d = 3), inside a curled remnant, on a seam (ends at different rungs), or touching a damaged point (d
   above 3)?
3. **Flaws per extra seed.** How many stuck curled pieces does each extra planted seed leave at the end of the opening,
   against T17's 0.288 ± 0.039 per extra seed, the same class of object on both sides (pieces at the curled rung, d = 1
   there and d = 2 here) and the planted flaws left out by position?
4. **The cooling shape** (reported, not scored): do the front-made leftovers fall with the cooling time as the columns did
   (as t^(−0.82 ± 0.13) in columns per sheet, the frozen share as t^(−0.34 ± 0.06); O104) or stay as the knots did?
   **Exponents only**: six-link fair sweeps are not T51's (section 5), the two clocks differ by 4/9, which is 0.35
   decades, so a decade ratio at the same nominal t is not comparable between the two toys and T51's R(10,000) and
   R(30,000) are not placed beside this test's.

## 6. Definitions, fixed now, read from the saved wiring (the code that exists is named; what is to be promoted is marked)

- **Rungs and regions.** d(v) = `graphity.dimension.local_dimension_d`, the number of link pairs at v that close no square
  (flat 3, the slab's rung 2, the rod's 1, a cube 0; above 3 damaged). A point is **open** (d = 3), **curled** (d ≤ 2) or
  **damaged** (d > 3). A link is placed by its two ends: **open** (both at 3), **curled** (both at 2 or below), a **seam**
  (ends at different rungs, neither damaged) or **touching damage**. Its squares by `cqg_d.squares_on_edge`: flat 4,
  **surplus** 5, **deficit** below 4. (`sandbox/slab_edge_and_flaws_3d.link_census_by_region`; to `graphity.objects`.)
- **Planted and front-made.** At sweep 0 the planted set P is the points at d > 3 (the joints), the ends of every
  five-square link and every deficit link lying inside the planted open region, and every point within one step of
  those. A flaw or piece at a later reading is **planted** if it has a point in P and **front-made** otherwise. Every
  count below is given for front-made objects (scored) and planted objects (reported). *Ours:* a front-made flaw within
  one step of P could still be the planted flaw moved by one step; the one-step margin is the compromise, and the counts
  within two steps are reported beside so a reader can see whether it matters.
- **The remnant column (1a):** a square whose four points are at d = 2, whose four links carry five squares, and whose
  every neighbor outside the square is at d = 3. **The open ring (1b):** a square whose four links carry five squares with
  all four points at d = 3 (`sandbox/curl_in_open_space.census`, `rings_open`; to `graphity.objects`). **Counted only if
  a dip:** every single switch touching any of its points costs energy at the run's λ (`sandbox/rod_relic_dip.py`'s
  test, the partner drawn from within a few steps of the piece as `analyse_t55.py` does with its NEAR; exact for the
  question asked, by the argument of `exact_walls_d.walls`). A ring that is not a dip is reported as a flicker. Each
  object is in the registration only if stage 0 (section 9, items 5 and 6) finds that a valid six-link graph can hold
  it as a dip; otherwise its line is struck and the question is settled without a run.
- **Leftover pieces:** the connected pieces of points not at d = 3 (`graphity.dimension.piece_labels`, the three-direction
  form of T37's and T51's leftovers). Each piece: its size; its d composition; **CURLED** if every point is at d ≤ 2
  (T17's class, one rung up; COLUMN if it is a remnant column); **DAMAGED** if any point is above 3; else **OTHER**; **a
  dip** or not (the test above); **far** if any link at its points has a way round of 7 or more (T55's seam reading,
  `explore_far_links.way_round`; 3 on a flat lattice); planted or front-made. A piece is **stuck** if it is a dip.
- **Energy above flat:** `cqg_d.hamiltonian(adj, λ)`, exact; the **frozen share** is that energy per point divided by the
  release per point a = 4(λ − 1) (1.6 at λ = 1.40, 1.0 at 1.25).
- **Width and wholeness:** the number of points within r steps of a point against the endless cubic lattice's
  1, 7, 25, 63, 129, ... ((2r + 1)(2r² + 2r + 3) / 3, exact; the three-direction form of O116's test, to be added to
  `scripts/read_endpoint_width.py` with a test on built tori), read from eight points per graph; and the pieces of the
  whole graph (`run_curled_bath_d.whole_pieces`): does the opening split the space (O100's question).
- **Seeds:** the planted k. Reported beside: the open regions of at least 16 points at d = 3 at the first reading with a
  tenth of the points at d = 3 (`run_curled_bath_d.open_regions_of` one rung up, T56's `open_regions3`), which says
  whether the bath seeded anything the planting did not.
- **Per slab and per extra seed:** a count summed over the followed replicas of a cell, divided by their number; the
  slope per extra seed is the difference of the k = 2 and k = 1 cell means at the same (λ, L) (two points, so the
  least-squares slope is the difference); standard errors from resampling replicas (2,000 resamples, the pairing kept,
  as `analyse_t51_amendment3.py`).
