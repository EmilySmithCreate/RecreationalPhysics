## How predictions are written from here (the owner's direction, same day)

## The targets

1. **Three large space directions, and time.** Needs: a birth that leaves exactly three directions open (her picture:
   the three are tied together, time perhaps a fourth). Now: six- and eight-link runs open directions one at a time and
   nothing selects three; the energy has no term that ties directions (O49, O50, O54, O62). Next: T39 and T40 (running);
   then a term that couples the directions (the loop-and-exchange brief's option B), tested against "three open, the rest
   curled".
2. **Gravity: attractive, falling as 1/r² in three directions, the same for all matter (to one part in 10¹⁵ for ordinary
   matter).** Needs: a mode with no energy gap, relics as its sources in proportion to their energy, a mode of the
   attracting kind (O60, O61). Now: this energy cannot give it (flat space is gapped); a field on the points would.
   Next: the owner's decision on that field; then two relics drifting together, the 1/r shape, and clumping.
3. **Dark matter: about 5.4 times the ordinary matter** (roughly 85 % of all matter). Needs: the scrap's share of the
   burp's energy, and how the number of seeds sets it. Now: T37 measures the energy left in scrap for the first time.
   Next: that share against λ and the seed density, set beside the ratio.
4. **A smooth, flat universe** (spatial curvature consistent with zero; the early universe uniform to about one part in
   10⁵). Needs: a birth from one seed or a few, not many (T37's pilots: many seeds leave a defected space). Now: T37.
   Next: the size of a clean region per seed, against λ, which would say how large a smooth universe one seed can make.
5. **Dark energy: a small positive vacuum energy, about 70 % of the total today.** Needs: whatever the burp leaves in the
   curvature of flat space; in the owner's picture (Updates 30, 32, 33), the third opening's release, held by space
   itself. Now: O70 fits a tie to the budget (two constants, two ratios) and O71 shows walls cannot set the shares. Next:
   T44 reads the release by order of opening under the follow tie (reported, not scored).
6. **Black holes** (observed: compact objects whose outsides match general relativity; the inside unobserved). Needs:
   concentrated energy re-curling space (her mechanism). Now: every test melts (O20, T21, T26, T27, T34), and a melt is
   only thermal excitation (O66). Next: T42 (counting near λ = 1.02, running); if gravity is adopted, clumps of relics.
