# Paper 1 quiz

Study aid, 2026-10-12. Take it after reading `paper1_explainers.md`. Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two;
a number where one is asked for. Paste your answers to Claude to have them graded, or write them down and check
against `paper1_answer_key.md` afterward. Do not open the key first.

Grading is on understanding, not wording. "I don't know" is a useful answer: it tells us what to explain again.

**A. The toy**

1. What does the dial λ change, and what is special about λ = 1?
2. The curled tube is at λ = 1.25. How much energy per point does the burp release? Where does that number come
   from?

**B. Barrier and waiting**

3. What is the barrier at λ = 1.25, and what does it physically correspond to (one of what)?
4. The wait at g = 1.5 is about 850 sweeps. Using only move A (cost 12, offered 3 times a sweep), roughly what
   does the wait become at g = 1.25? (Use e ≈ 2.718 and e^1.6 ≈ 5.)
5. Someone says: "Bigger tubes have more places to start, so they should convert sooner." The paper's measurements
   at N = 64, 96, 144, 192 say what? And is the explanation a fact about nature or about the chain?
6. At λ = 4/3, something changes about which move is the cheapest exit. What?

**C. Waiting-time statistics**

7. A tube has already waited 2,000 sweeps without leaving. If the wait is memoryless, how long do you expect
   it to wait from now, compared with a fresh tube?
8. The pre-registered test said "CV between 0.7 and 1.3", and it failed in some cells. Why is that not strong
   evidence against a memoryless wait?

**D. Front and "sharp"**

9. In plain words, what is a front, and what number tells you the change is "two orders with almost nothing in between"?
10. About a third of first exits return to the tube. What is that fraction called, and what does it say about the
    barrier?
11. A listener says: "So you found a first-order phase transition." What do you say?

**E. Sealed system**

12. Why is the smallest seed that works exactly 12, and why is that a statement about *this* dynamics as well as
    about energy?
13. With only a few stores the converted region melts. In one sentence, why?

**F. Relic and interchangeable points**

14. The paper reports about one relic per tube at every size 64 to 288. Under rule 14, what would be needed before
    you could say "the relic does not grow with size"?
15. If vertices are treated as interchangeable, which of these change: ε, the barrier, the 12-unit seed, the
    waiting time? Which has been run?

**G. Kinds of statement (rule 10)**

Label each one: exact, measured (pre-registered), measured (exploratory), interpretation, or the hypothesis's claim.

16. "The tube sits 4(λ − 1) per point above the flat grid."
17. "The waiting time follows an Arrhenius law with nothing fitted."
18. "About a third of exits recross, which we read as a transmission coefficient."
19. "Space emerged from a specific ordered precursor through a sharp change of this kind."

**H. The one a physicist will ask**

20. "Is this chain the physical time evolution of anything?" What is the honest answer, and which results survive
    if the answer is no?
