- 2026-10-11, ours, what that idea touches (general knowledge, not read by us, to verify before any of it is
  cited). (1) Along a light ray the distance in space and the lapse of time cancel exactly: its length in
  spacetime is zero and a clock riding on it would not tick. (2) In a light wave the electric and magnetic parts
  cancel in both of the combinations every observer agrees on. (3) Kaluza and Klein: light as the part of gravity
  that points along one extra, curled direction, with electric charge as motion round it. (4) In quantum mechanics
  the wave of two particles lives in a space of six dimensions and of three particles in nine, three per
  particle, and identical particles are not told apart there until something interacts with them. (5) Penrose's
  twistors take light rays as the basic points; the light rays of our spacetime form a five-dimensional space
  inside a six-dimensional one. (6) Feynman's sum over paths: every path counts, each weighted by its length
  measured in wavelengths, and probabilities come from the sum: count-like, length-of-loop-like and time-like at
  once. The record's caution stands beside it: counting the versions of one fixed arrangement cannot reproduce the
  quantum correlations (T15 rung 3a); a view from more dimensions is a different proposal and has no test here yet.
